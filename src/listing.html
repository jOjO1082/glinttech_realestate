Listing Steps

Step 1

Send Us Your Property Details

  • Property type (apartment, bungalow, office, land, etc.)
  • Number of bedrooms/bathrooms
  • Size (in sqm, plot, or acres)
  • Location (State → LGA → Street)
  • Price and payment terms
  • High-quality images & videos (optional but recommended)

Be as detailed as possible. Clear information boosts your listing's credibility.

Step 2

Provide Verification Documents

  • Certificate of Occupancy (C of O)
  • Deed of Assignment / Conveyance
  • Survey Plan
  • Governor's Consent (if available)
  • Proof of Ownership / Utility Bill
  • Valid ID of Owner or Authorized Agent

These documents help us verify authenticity and protect buyers and sellers.

Step 3

Submit Owner / Agent Contact Info

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email
  • Relationship to property (Owner / Agent / Family Representative)

We only use this information to confirm the listing and connect serious buyers.

Step 4

Verification & Approval

  • Our team reviews your documents
  • Confirms property location via digital checks
  • Requests additional proof if needed
  • Approves and prepares your listing

We make sure only real, legitimate listings go live.

Step 5

Your Property Goes Live

  • Your property appears on the website
  • It becomes discoverable to buyers
  • You receive inquiries directly

We keep your listing active as long as the information remains accurate and valid.
